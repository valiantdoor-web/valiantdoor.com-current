Common East Dublin repairs

Most East Dublin calls fall into a few buckets: a broken torsion spring that leaves the door too heavy to lift, a door that has come off-track or is binding, or an opener that will not respond or reverses on close. Because many homes here were built around the same time, we often see the same springs and rollers wearing out across a neighborhood.

Fast Diagnosis Checklist

Tell Valiant whether the door is stuck open, stuck closed, heavy, crooked, off-track, noisy, or showing opener error flashes. Photos of the spring line, cable drums, and opener lights help route the call.
