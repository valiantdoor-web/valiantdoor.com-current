When A Garage Door Problem Is An Emergency

The Door Will Not Secure

A door stuck open can leave the home or business exposed. Valiant prioritizes secure-close requests and doors that cannot be operated safely.

A Spring, Cable, Or Track Failed

Broken springs, loose or snapped cables, crooked sections, and off-track rollers can allow the door to fall or bind. Stop operating the door and keep people clear.

A Vehicle Is Trapped

Tell us whether the door is stuck open or closed, whether a vehicle is trapped, and whether any hardware is hanging or visibly damaged so we can triage the request accurately.

Emergency Garage Door Repair Questions

When should I call for emergency garage door repair?

Call for urgent service when the door will not close, will not open safely, is off-track, or has a broken spring or cable. Valiant answers calls 24/7; appointments at 7:00 PM or later carry a flat $100 after-hours service charge before approved work.

What should I do before the technician arrives?

Stop operating the door if a spring or cable is broken, the door is crooked, or a section is hanging. Keep people and vehicles clear, do not pull the emergency release on an unstable door, and send photos from a safe distance when booking.

How quickly can Valiant respond?

Valiant provides 24/7 phone intake and prioritizes doors that will not secure, trapped-vehicle situations, broken springs, off-track doors, and unsafe hanging sections. Arrival time depends on route position, traffic, technician availability, and parts.

Is there an after-hours service charge?

Appointments scheduled for 7:00 PM or later carry a flat $100 after-hours service charge before any approved parts or repair work. Valiant explains the repair options and itemized price before work begins.

Do you provide same-day appointments?

Yes. Same-day scheduling is available when technician, route, and parts availability allow.

Do you service residential and commercial doors?

Yes. Valiant handles residential garage doors and many light-commercial door service requests. Call with the door type, opening size, and problem so the request can be triaged correctly.
