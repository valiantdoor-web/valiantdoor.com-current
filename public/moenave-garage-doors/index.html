The system

What Is a Trackless Pivot Garage Door?

A conventional sectional door rides through vertical and horizontal tracks, bends at each hinge line, and parks beneath the ceiling. A trackless pivot system removes the horizontal ceiling tracks and ceiling-mounted operator.

Compatible insulated sectional panels are joined into one rigid door leaf. Wall and header hardware control its travel, torsion springs counterbalance the finished weight, and a wall-mounted LiftMaster 98022 drives the system.

The result is a clean ceiling and a single architectural plane. It can preserve room for vehicle lifts, storage, gym equipment, lighting, or exposed structure while supporting flush steel, overlay, full-view glass, and compatible woodgrain-plank designs.

Clearance comes first

How the Door Moves

Removing overhead track does not remove the need for clearance. It relocates that clearance into a controlled rotational path.

Outward arc

Per current Moenave specifications, roughly half the door height swings outward. An 8-foot door projects about 4 feet into the driveway during travel.

Interior arc

The upper portion rotates inward by roughly the same amount. Lighting, shelving, lifts, beams, and other equipment must stay clear of that envelope.

Open position

The door occupies about 6 inches of the opening when fully open. An 8-foot opening therefore provides roughly 7 feet 6 inches of clear height.

Opening geometry

The finished opening is approximately 2 inches wider than the panel width. A 16-foot door uses a 16-foot 2-inch finished opening.

Prequalification

Is Your Garage a Candidate?

Most disqualifiers can be identified from measurements and a few clear photographs before a truck rolls.

Straightforward candidates

  • 95 inches or more from floor to ceiling
  • 12 inches of clear headroom
  • Square or rectangular opening
  • Clear driveway apron through the outward arc
  • New construction or an opening that can be resized
  • Total finished weight comfortably below 500 lb

Needs additional review

  • Arched opening
  • Ribbed interior panel face, including WD 9100 or 9605
  • Unverified panel construction or thickness
  • Insufficient headroom or ceiling height
  • Grade, vehicles, walkways, or fixtures in the swing path
  • Plans to reuse conventionally sized panels
  • Heavy cladding, glass, or struts near the weight limit
  • High-wind or special code requirements
  • Framing unable to support the current hardware geometry

Finding the constraint before fabrication is our Proof Before Proposal™ standard.

Direct answers

Trackless Pivot Garage Door FAQ

What is a trackless pivot garage door?

Standard sectional panels are locked together into one rigid leaf that rotates outward and upward on wall-mounted pivot hardware, rather than folding and rolling along overhead ceiling track. There is no horizontal track and no ceiling-mounted motor. A wall-mounted jackshaft operator drives the door and torsion springs counterbalance its weight.

How much headroom does a trackless pivot garage door require?

12 inches above the opening, for the torsion spring assembly and the top-of-door arc, per current Moenave published specifications.

What ceiling height is required?

A minimum of 95 inches floor to ceiling, applying across all supported door heights from 7 to 10 feet.

What door sizes are supported?

8 to 18 feet wide and 7 to 10 feet tall, with a maximum total door weight of 500 pounds. The hardware adds approximately 70 pounds on an 8-foot door, counting toward that 500-pound maximum.

Can I reuse my existing garage door panels?

Usually not. The pivot system requires a finished opening approximately 2 inches wider than the panels, while conventional panels are sized to fill the opening. Most projects use new, correctly dimensioned panels.

What garage door panels are compatible?

Triple-layer insulated sectional panels with a flat interior face, including flush steel, composite overlay, aluminum glass, and most woodgrain plank styles. Panels with interior ribbing are not compatible, including the Wayne Dalton 9100 and 9605 series. Exact panel thickness compatibility is project-specific and is verified against current Moenave hardware and technical requirements before ordering.

Does the finished opening need to be wider than the door panels?

Yes, approximately 2 inches wider. A 16-foot door requires a 16-foot 2-inch finished opening.

Does a trackless pivot garage door swing outward?

Yes. Roughly half the door height travels outward and roughly half inward. An 8-foot door swings approximately 4 feet outside and 4 feet inside the opening, and occupies about 6 inches of the opening when fully open.

Can a trackless pivot door work with an arched garage opening?

Square and rectangular openings are the standard supported geometry. An arched opening can restrict travel and may prevent full operation, so it requires project-specific review.

What garage door opener does the trackless pivot system use?

A LiftMaster 98022 wall-mount jackshaft operator. Moenave states that it has not tested the 98032, which would require different mounting hardware not included with the system.

Can integrated RGBW lighting be added?

Moenave publishes an integrated RGBW LED system with a dedicated white channel that connects to the LiftMaster 98022, activates with door operation, shuts off automatically, mounts vertically or horizontally, runs on standard 110-volt power, and is configured from a phone without a dedicated app.

How long does a trackless pivot garage door take to order?

Hardware is built to order for the project's dimensions. Current production and shipping schedules vary. Valiant confirms manufacturer lead time when the project is configured and quoted.

How much does a complete installed trackless pivot garage door cost?

Pricing is quoted per project after measurement. Cost depends on the opening, panels, finish, glass or cladding, spring engineering, operator, lighting, framing, electrical work, delivery, and site conditions.

Is the trackless pivot system hurricane or Miami-Dade rated?

No. Moenave states the trackless pivot hardware system is not currently tested or certified for hurricane or high-wind building code requirements, including Miami-Dade approvals. Panel wind performance must still meet local code.

Yes. Valiant Garage Door is listed in Moenave's installer network for Northern California and provides trackless pivot system planning and installation throughout the Tri-Valley and surrounding Bay Area, including ongoing service after installation.
