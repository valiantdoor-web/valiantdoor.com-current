What steps are involved in professional garage door spring replacement?

A professional secures the door, releases remaining spring energy, confirms the correct replacement size, inspects the shaft, bearings, drums, and cables, installs and tensions the spring, then balances and cycle-tests the complete door.

What to watch for

  • Secure the door and shaft before changing spring tension.
  • Match spring size to door weight and operating geometry.
  • Inspect cables, drums, bearings, and end plates before reassembly.
  • Finish with balance, travel, and safety-reversal testing.
Safety note: Torsion springs store enough energy to cause severe injury. This footage documents a professional procedure and is not a homeowner instruction to attempt the repair.
