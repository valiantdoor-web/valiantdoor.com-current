Those are real starting prices—not a promise that every door costs the same. Door weight, spring engineering, cycle rating, access, conversion needs, and approved safety corrections determine the final written price.

The Six Factors That Legitimately Change Spring Cost

  1. Door weight and construction. Insulated steel, wood, custom, and oversized doors require different spring engineering.
  2. One spring or a matched pair. Two-spring doors should be evaluated as a system because both springs share cycle history.
  3. Cycle rating. Higher-cycle designs use more material and are intended for doors that operate frequently.
  4. Existing system type. TorqueMaster, extension-spring, hi-lift, and specialty systems can require conversion hardware.
  5. Connected damage. Cables, drums, bearings, bottom fixtures, and shaft condition are inspected—not automatically replaced.
  6. Timing and access. After-hours service, restricted access, or a trapped vehicle can affect dispatch and labor.

Should You Replace Both Springs?

On a two-spring door, the pair has normally carried the same cycles. Replacing only the broken spring may leave an equally fatigued spring in service and can make balancing less predictable. Valiant explains the measured condition and gives the customer the choice before work begins; no connected part is treated as automatically “required” without evidence.

Why Spring Replacement Is Not A DIY Price Comparison

Torsion and extension springs store enough energy to lift a heavy door repeatedly. A slipping winding bar, unsecured shaft, wrong spring, incorrect winding direction, or disturbed cable drum can cause severe injury. The professional work includes controlled tension handling, correct spring selection, system inspection, balance, travel, cable, and opener-force checks.

Spring Pricing Questions

How much does Valiant charge for garage door spring replacement?

Valiant Garage Door currently lists single-spring installation starting at $159 and double-spring installation starting at $189. These are starting prices, not universal flat quotes. Final pricing depends on door weight, spring size, cycle rating, access, conversion needs, and any approved safety corrections.

Should both garage door springs be replaced together?

On a two-spring door, replacing the matched pair is usually the better long-term repair because both springs have carried the same cycle history. The technician should still inspect and explain the system before the customer approves the work.

What warranty does Valiant provide on springs?

Qualifying spring replacement work includes a 10-year warranty. Qualifying installed repair parts can carry a lifetime parts warranty, and qualifying installed garage door openers carry a 5-year warranty. Exact coverage is documented on the estimate or invoice.

Why can the final spring price be higher than the starting price?

Heavier or custom doors require different spring engineering. High-cycle upgrades, two-spring conversions, damaged cables or bearings, Wayne Dalton TorqueMaster conversions, hi-lift systems, and after-hours appointments can also change the approved total.

Is garage door spring replacement safe to do yourself?

No. Torsion and extension springs store enough energy to cause severe injury. Spring sizing, tension release, winding, cable control, balance testing, and final safety testing should be completed by a trained technician with the correct tools.
