Why This Pleasanton Garage Door Spring Failed After Only 5 Years

Last Updated August 5, 2026 · By Valentino Ramirez

A properly sized and installed torsion spring should last 7 to 10 years, sometimes longer. When a Pleasanton homeowner's spring gave out after only 5, the cause turned out to be hardware the original installer never leveled correctly. Here is what we found, and what any homeowner can learn from it.

The Call

What The Homeowner Reported

The garage door spring snapped and the door would no longer lift safely. The homeowner mentioned it had only been about 5 years since the door and opener were installed, which immediately stood out as unusually early for a torsion spring to fail.

Same-Day Response

A neighbor referred the homeowner to Valiant Garage Door, and we scheduled a same-day appointment within the promised window.

What We Found On Inspection

A Severely Crooked End Plate

The end plate, the bracket that anchors the shaft and bearing at the end of the torsion spring system, was badly out of level instead of mounted flush and square.

An Incredibly Crooked Spring Plate

A few inches away, the spring plate the spring itself seats against was also badly out of level, which is very dangerous since a spring under tension against misaligned hardware can slip or fail unpredictably.

A Leftover Installation Mistake

This was not normal wear. It was a leftover mistake from the original installation, and it explains why the spring only lasted 5 years instead of the 7 to 10 (sometimes up to 12) years a correctly installed system typically delivers.

What This Means For Other Homeowners

Spring Lifespan Is Not Guaranteed By Age Alone

A correctly sized and installed torsion spring should typically last 7 to 10 years, sometimes up to 12. If yours fails much sooner, installation quality, not just normal wear, is worth investigating.

Level Hardware Matters As Much As The Spring Itself

Crooked end plates and spring plates put constant uneven stress on the spring and bearings. Fixing only the spring without correcting the hardware underneath just sets up the next early failure.

We Check The Whole System, Not Just The Broken Part

Every mounting point along the shaft gets checked with a level before we sign off on a repair, so the new hardware seats correctly instead of repeating the same mistake.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why Did My Garage Door Spring Break After Only A Few Years?

A correctly sized and installed torsion spring should typically last 7 to 10 years, sometimes up to 12. If a spring fails much earlier, the usual cause is installation error, such as a crooked end plate or spring plate that puts uneven, constant extra stress on the spring until it gives out.

How Can I Tell If My Garage Door Was Installed Incorrectly?

Warning signs include springs or hardware wearing out earlier than expected, a door that feels unbalanced, unusual noise during operation, or visibly crooked brackets and plates along the torsion shaft. A technician can check level and alignment at every mounting point during an inspection.

Can Broken Spring Repair Usually Happen The Same Day In Pleasanton?

In many cases yes, provided the schedule and the correct spring size are available. After replacement, the door should be rebalanced and safety tested before normal use.

Do You Fix Mistakes Left By A Previous Garage Door Installer?

Yes. When we find misaligned hardware, incorrect spring sizing, or other leftover installation errors, we correct them as part of the repair so the same failure does not happen again.
