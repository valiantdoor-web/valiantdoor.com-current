Garage Door Problems We Fix Same-Day In Pleasanton

Broken Springs And Heavy Doors

If the door feels suddenly heavy, stops halfway, or slams shut, a broken torsion spring is usually the cause. We stock common spring sizes on the truck so most Pleasanton spring failures are fixed the same day.

Doors Stuck Open Or Closed

A door stuck open leaves your home exposed and a door stuck closed can trap your car inside. Both are top-priority same-day calls that we work to reach as quickly as the schedule allows.

Snapped Cables And Off-Track Doors

Frayed cables, cables off the drum, and off-track doors make the door unsafe to operate. These are stop-using-it-now situations we prioritize for same-day repair before the door drops or jams.

Opener Failures

When the opener hums but the door will not move, the cause is often a spring or travel-force issue rather than a dead motor. We diagnose the real fault the same day instead of guessing.

Failed Safety Sensors

A door that reverses or refuses to close often has a misaligned or failed photo-eye sensor. We realign or replace sensors and confirm safe auto-reverse before we leave.
