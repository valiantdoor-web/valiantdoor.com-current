What Is A Wayne-Dalton TorqueMaster Or EZ-Set System?

Enclosed Spring, Proprietary Parts

Wayne-Dalton built TorqueMaster and EZ-Set systems to enclose the torsion spring inside a round steel tube mounted above the door, instead of an exposed spring on a standard torsion bar. It was marketed as a safer, cleaner-looking system, but the springs, winding cones, and internal hardware are proprietary to Wayne-Dalton. As those parts age out or become harder to source, a failed TorqueMaster tube can leave a door stuck with no simple like-for-like fix.

Why Convert To Standard Torsion?

Serviceable Parts

A standard torsion bar, springs, cable drums, and bearing plates can be serviced by any qualified technician using widely stocked hardware — no more waiting on a single proprietary part.

Springs Built To Last Longer

We install DuraMaster powder-coated torsion springs from Service Spring Corp, made in the USA and rated to last roughly 3x longer than the factory Wayne-Dalton TorqueMaster spring, which is a single-use spring never designed to be rebuilt or repaired once it fails.

Future-Proofed

Once converted, future spring replacements, cable repairs, and balance adjustments are simple, standard jobs instead of a search for discontinued TorqueMaster components.
